# L1.3 follow-up: per-address TOTP budget (2026-11-08)

Closes "per-account TOTP budget can lock out an operator whose password is known" from `l1.3-account-secret-safety-2026-10-26.md`.

## Change
- Login second factor now spends two budgets: per (account, client address) 8 failures / 15 min, and a per-account ceiling of 24 / 15 min (`src/lib/identity/mfa.ts`). `/api/auth` passes `getClientIp`. A success clears both. Callers without an address keep the old strict 8 / 15 min per-account budget (enrollment confirmation unchanged).
- Exhaustion of either budget logs a `[mfa]` warning with account and address (the alert hook; no pager integration).

## Verification
- `tests/repository/platform-mfa.test.ts` +3 cases (other address still succeeds; many addresses hit the ceiling; success clears budgets). 118 tests in the 11 files touching the auth route / MFA pass on a throwaway Postgres; tsc and eslint clean.
- Review: lead-only (no independent security review run).

## Limits
- Brute-force headroom per account rises from 8 to 24 guesses / 15 min (about 3 in 10^6 each; still negligible against 6 digits plus single-use steps).
- A distributed attacker with the password can still reach the ceiling and block the account for up to 15 min, and the per-address key is only as trustworthy as the client address: without `TRUSTED_PROXY_HOPS` an attacker can rotate spoofed addresses (the L2.1 edge contract, still open).
