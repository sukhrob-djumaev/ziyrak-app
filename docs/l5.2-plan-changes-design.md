# L5.2 design note - plan changes and over-limit downgrades (B05)

Status: proposal for L5.2, written before code as required by `l5-payment-lifecycle-design.md` point 11. Extends that design; it changes no L5.1 behaviour. Stripe test mode only.

## 1. Change kinds and timing

| Change | Effective | Provider call |
|---|---|---|
| Add an add-on, raise a quantity, switch to a dearer bundle | Now, once the provider confirms payment | `changeSubscription` with `payment_behavior=pending_if_incomplete`; entitlements follow the confirmed apply, never the request |
| Remove an add-on, lower a quantity, switch to a cheaper bundle | End of the paid period | None now. The item is marked `scheduled_remove`; a Ziyrak job applies it at period end |
| Cancel | End of the paid period (`canceling`) | `cancelSubscription(period_end)` |

A failed or unpaid upgrade changes nothing: the provider keeps the old items, the operation moves to `failed`, no entitlement is granted.

## 2. Quotes, staleness and concurrency

A change quote is bound to the subscription `rev` and the catalog versions, with the same 15-minute hash rule as checkout. If `rev` moved (another change, a webhook apply) the confirm fails with `QUOTE_STALE`. Changes take the tenant write lock, allow one non-final `change` operation per workspace (a second returns 409), and use the operation's idempotency key for the provider call.

## 3. Which limits can be exceeded

Only `limit` capabilities can be over-used. Today that is `seats.members`; every other capability is boolean. Removing a boolean capability stops the feature at the effective time and keeps its data and settings inert; nothing is deleted. Overlapping grants already resolve as a union of active purchases, so removing one source retains the other. `requiresHumanApproval` rules are independent of the plan and never relax.

## 4. Seat reduction below current usage

Usage counts members plus open invitations, as the invitation service does.

1. **Scheduling is allowed**, and the owner sees a conflict list: "N members, new limit M".
2. **The owner chooses who stays** (the owner membership always stays). Nothing is chosen for them.
3. **At the effective time**: if the chosen set fits, the provider change is applied and the unchosen members are suspended (see 5). If it does not fit, see 4.
4. **Unresolved at the effective time**: the reduction is not applied. The old quantity stays and keeps billing, the scheduled removal is cancelled, and the owner is told why. No member is suspended without a choice and no access is lost by inaction. Re-scheduling is allowed at any time.

This is the explicit expiry policy the spec asks for: a pending reduction that is not resolved lapses instead of being forced.

## 5. Suspending members

`Membership` has no active flag today. L5.2 adds a nullable `suspendedByPlanAt` column (expand-only migration, covered by the migration-compat guard). A suspended member cannot sign in to the workspace and holds no seat; their records, conversations and history stay. Choosing them again (after the limit rises) clears it. Each suspension and restore is written to `BillingOperation` detail for audit.

## 6. Failure, grace and recovery (B06)

Unchanged from the design: `past_due` keeps access inside `graceEndsAt`, set once and never extended; expiry ends the subscription and the payment hold applies; a confirmed payment restores access. L5.2 adds the grace-expiry job, the reconcile job (every 10 minutes), the `repair_provision` operator action, and alerts.

## 7. Tests (written first)

- Upgrade applies only after confirmed payment; a failed upgrade grants nothing.
- Stale quote and two concurrent changes: one wins, one gets 409/`QUOTE_STALE`.
- A scheduled removal keeps access until period end, then removes exactly that item's grants.
- Removing one of two overlapping grants keeps the other; approvals cannot be bypassed.
- Seat reduction: conflict list is correct, the owner cannot be suspended, an unresolved reduction lapses with the old quantity intact, a resolved one suspends exactly the unchosen members, and nothing is deleted.
- Cross-workspace: no change, conflict or suspension touches another workspace.

## 8. Open for the owner

Nothing blocks coding. Two values are policy data, not code: the quote lifetime (15 minutes) and whether to notify by email on a lapsed reduction (uses the existing mail path).
