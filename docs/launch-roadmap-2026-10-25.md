# Self-service launch roadmap — 2026-10-25

## Decision, scope and evidence

Decision **LP-2026-10-25** records the owner's request to refresh documentation, restart delivery from the current project, review dashboard/access/usability, and support configurable subscriptions. It selects launch preparation, not deployment or live charging. The intended outcome is a customer who can register, verify email, understand the offer, pay, set up a supported channel and start work without manual platform provisioning.

Keep the modular monolith, tenant boundary, provider contracts, separate worker and existing account foundation. Do not restart the migration or discard prior work. The [October 25 review](project-review-2026-10-25.md) is the defect/evidence baseline on product commit `5b45bd7`; documentation baseline is `3066b33`. The [dashboard review](dashboard-and-access-review-2026-10-25.md) and [billing contract](billing-and-entitlements-spec.md) specify the next experience. These documents describe requirements, not shipped billing or passed acceptance.

This amendment affects original architecture §§7–12, 15–16, 23–25, 33–39 and 44: commercial access becomes an additional admission condition, subscriptions gain their own records/adapter, and acceptance includes the commercial lifecycle. It lifts the billing deferral only for the bounded catalog/subscription scope below. Security, tenant/resource isolation, support grants and module dependency direction remain mandatory. New channels, marketplace/customer payment collection, enterprise storage/cutover and a general billing rules engine remain outside scope.

## Minimum launch scope and useful additions

Deliver one reliable Web Chat path first and Meta WhatsApp only with its required provider/setup proof. Keep knowledge, inbox/handoff, customers, account recovery/invitations, clear usage and owner billing. Keep useful ticket/automation/SLA features accessible without making every advanced screen a primary menu item. Do not advertise Email/Phone/demo QR or Keyword Alert as paid production capabilities.

The launch requirements beyond checkout are: verified transactional mail, privileged-account protection, signup/trial abuse controls and platform spend ceilings, clear usage/limit behavior, customer recovery/help, privacy/retention/export/deletion procedures, honest service status, accessible essential journeys and accountable operations. Publish actual terms/privacy/refund/support information before collecting live money; specific legal/tax content depends on the seller and markets, so this roadmap does not invent it.

Defer broad channel expansion, enterprise infrastructure, elaborate workflow builders, marketplace payments, arbitrary metered overages and many pricing experiments. A payment system cannot compensate for unreliable service or unexplained access limits. Conversely, it is reasonable to build the catalog and sandbox billing during launch preparation once their safety dependencies are ready; production revenue does not need to wait for every possible feature.

## Release decision

**Today: no unrestricted paid launch.** A controlled unpaid/staging run can be useful under its own safe topology. Paid pilot readiness requires L1–L6 evidence and explicit L7 authorization. These are bounded gates, not a demand for a perfect product.

Before L7, demonstrate: new account → verified email → understandable package/price → confirmed payment → correct access → knowledge → actual supported-channel customer reply → human handoff; plus payment failure, duplicate/delayed events, cancelled subscription, revoked membership, recovery and support. Distinguish assistant-only test, provider API acceptance and recipient-visible delivery. No customer should pay for a channel that still requires undisclosed operator work or third-party approval.
